University Rent & Lend – DormShare




Introduction:





University Rent & Lend is a web application designed to allow students living in university accommodation to rent and lend items to one another. The main idea behind the application is to help students save money by sharing items they only need for a short period of time, rather than having to buy them. Many students spend money on items that they might only use once or twice. For example, a student might need a screwdriver, a vacuum cleaner, a printer, a cooking appliance or a particular piece of equipment for a short period. Instead of buying these items, students can use the application to find another student who is willing to lend them.
The application creates a small sharing community within university accommodation. Students can list items they own but do not regularly use, allowing other students to rent them using virtual coins. These coins can be earned through different activities on the application, such as lending items to other students. This creates a system where students are encouraged to help each other while also receiving rewards.
The overall aim is to make student life more affordable, reduce unnecessary spending and encourage students to reuse and share items instead of constantly buying new ones.


The Problem:

Living at university can be expensive, especially for students who have to pay for accommodation, food, transport and other everyday expenses. Students often need different household items throughout the academic year, but buying everything themselves can become expensive.
For example, a student might need a drill to put something together, a printer to print coursework or a suitcase for a weekend trip. These items might only be needed for a few hours or days. Buying something that will only be used occasionally can be a waste of money.
Another problem is that many students already own items that they rarely use. These items might sit unused in their accommodation while another student living in the same building is spending money buying the exact same thing.
University Rent & Lend aims to solve this problem by connecting students who need items with students who already own them. Instead of everyone buying their own items, students can share what they have.



How it Works:


The application will be a web-based platform that students can access through their computers, tablets or mobile phones.
Students will first create an account and log in. They will be able to create a personal profile containing basic information, such as their name, university email address and accommodation details.
Once logged in, students can browse available items listed by other students. Items will be organised into different categories to make searching easier. Examples of categories include:
 	Kitchen equipment (toasters, kettles and air fryers).
 	Cleaning equipment (vacuum cleaners, mops and irons).
 	Technology (chargers, keyboards and monitors).
 	Tools (screwdrivers, drills and toolkits).
 	Travel items (suitcases and travel accessories).
 	Sports and leisure equipment.
When a student finds something, they need, they can select the item to view more information. This will include an image, description, rental price in coins, availability and the student who owns it.
The student can then request to rent the item. The owner can accept or reject the request. If accepted, the students can arrange when and where the item will be collected and returned.
Once the rental is completed, the coins are transferred to the owner.
Gamification and Virtual Coin System:
One of the main features of the application is its gamification system. Gamification means using game-like features, such as points, rewards, levels and achievements, to encourage people to use an application.
Instead of using real money, students will use virtual coins to rent items. This means students do not need to connect bank accounts or make payments through the application.
Students can earn coins by lending their belongings to other students. For example, if a student lends their vacuum cleaner to someone for two days, they could receive 10 coins once the rental is completed.
The coins earned can then be used to rent other items from different students. This creates a system where students benefit from helping one another.
The application could also include additional rewards to make the experience more interesting. For example:
 	Levels: Students can progress through different levels depending on how many successful rentals they complete.
 	Badges: Students can earn badges for lending items regularly or helping other students.
 	Achievements: Rewards could be given for completing their first rental or lending five different items.
 	Leaderboards: Students could see who has earned the most coins or completed the most rentals.
These features are designed to make sharing more enjoyable and encourage students to participate in the community.


Main Features:


The application will contain several important features.
User registration and login: Students will be able to create accounts and securely log in. University email verification could be used to help make sure that users belong to the university community.
User profiles: Each student will have a profile showing their name, profile picture, available coins, items listed and reviews from other students.
List an item: Students can upload pictures of their belongings, write descriptions, choose a category and set the number of coins required to rent the item.
Search and filtering: Students will be able to search for specific items and filter results by category, coin price and availability.
Rent an item: Students can request items from other users using their available coins. The owner can accept or reject rental requests.
Coin wallet: Each student will have a virtual wallet showing how many coins they have earned and spent.
Messaging: Students could communicate with one another to arrange collection times, discuss items and organise returns.
Ratings and reviews: After a rental is completed, students can leave ratings and reviews. This will help build trust and allow users to see which students have a good history of lending and borrowing items.
Rental history: Students will be able to see their previous rentals, current requests and items they have lent to others.


Benefits:


The application offers several benefits to students living in university accommodation.
Firstly, it helps students save money. Instead of buying items they only need occasionally, they can rent them from other students using virtual coins.
Secondly, it encourages students to make better use of the belongings they already own. Items that would normally sit unused can be shared with others who need them.
Thirdly, it helps create a stronger student community. Students can interact with people living in the same accommodation and help each other with everyday needs.
Another benefit is that the application encourages students to think about sustainability. Buying fewer unnecessary items can help reduce waste, packaging and the demand for new products. Sharing items allows existing products to be used more often instead of being thrown away or forgotten.
Safety and Trust:
As students will be lending personal belongings to other people, safety and trust are important parts of the application.
The platform could use university email verification to make sure that users are genuine students. Profiles, ratings and reviews will also help users understand who they are renting from.
Rental requests will require approval from the item owner before an item can be borrowed. Both students will be able to view the rental details, including the agreed collection and return dates.
The application could also include a reporting feature so users can report inappropriate behaviour, damaged items or users who do not return borrowed belongings.
These features will help create a safer and more reliable sharing environment.


Overall Aim:
The overall aim of University Rent & Lend is to create a convenient and affordable way for university students to share everyday items within their accommodation.
The application provides an alternative to buying items that students might only need temporarily. By introducing virtual coins and gamification, it encourages students to lend their belongings and earn rewards rather than allowing useful items to go unused.
Ultimately, the application is about saving money, reducing waste and building a helpful student community. It allows students to benefit from what others already own while giving them an opportunity to share their own belongings in return.
